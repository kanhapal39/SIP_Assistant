# CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

The average yearly rate at which an investment would have grown from its starting value to its ending value, assuming it grew at a steady, compounding rate each year. It suits a single lump-sum investment held over a period, since there's one start date and one amount invested. A SIP is made up of many instalments invested on different dates, so CAGR doesn't accurately reflect how a SIP has performed — XIRR is used instead, because it accounts for the timing of each instalment.
