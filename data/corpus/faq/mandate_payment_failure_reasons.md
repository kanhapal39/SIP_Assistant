# Why would my SIP payment fail even with sufficient balance?

A SIP debit can fail for several reasons beyond low balance: the mandate not yet being approved/activated by the bank, the UPI AutoPay daily or per-transaction limit being exceeded, a temporary issue with the bank's NACH processing system, or the mandate having expired or been revoked. A failed instalment is typically just skipped for that cycle — the next month's instalment proceeds normally once the underlying issue clears.
